01 The problem

Two margin leaks Shopify doesn't fix natively.

Excluding products from discount codes isn't a native setting, and quantity caps aren't either. Merchants either build fragile workarounds or lose money finding out the hard way.

"

A 15% code stacks on a product that's already on sale. You find the double discount in your margin report — after the order shipped.

The double-discount disaster
"

Merchants have filed feature requests on the Shopify forums for a "not eligible for discount codes" checkbox. It still doesn't exist.

No native exclusion setting
"

A customer orders 6 giant plushies on flat-rate shipping. Past a certain quantity, the parcel jumps a carrier price tier — and the order loses money.

The shipping-margin problem
"

A limited edition drops. Without a per-order cap, resellers empty the entire stock in one checkout before real customers get a chance.

02 What makes CartRules different

Built around the one combo merchants actually need.

Six principles behind CartRules — each one addresses a specific gap in the discount-code-exclusion and order-limit apps already on the App Store.

01 — TWO RULES, ONE APP

Discounts and quantity, together.

Order-limit apps don't touch discounts. Discount suites don't touch quantity. CartRules does both, in one tiny app, at a fraction of the price.

02 — 3-STEP WIZARD

Native Polaris. No clutter.

Rule type → products → message. Three screens, no settings maze. Built for a busy, non-technical merchant, not a power user.

03 — YOUR OWN WORDS

A custom message per rule.

A limit without explanation looks like a bug. Write the customer-facing message yourself, in any language — French, Japanese, anything.

04 — TARGET LIKE YOU THINK

Product, collection, or tag.

Tag a rule once — no-promo, heavy-item — and every future product with that tag is covered automatically.

05 — POLITE, NOT BROKEN

Corrected, never blocked blind.

Quantity auto-adjusts in the cart with a clear note. A blocked discount code shows your message instead of a generic checkout error.

06 — FLAT PRICE

No revenue share, ever.

One flat monthly price. We undercut the category leader while doing the one combo it explicitly refuses to build.

06 What's next

On the roadmap.

V2 ideas we're planning after v1 is live on the App Store. Built around what merchants actually ask for — no fixed dates.

Planned

Per-customer limits across orders

Cap total quantity per customer across multiple orders, not just within a single cart — closes the "reorder to bypass" loophole.

Planned

Customer-tag exceptions

Let wholesale or VIP customer tags bypass a quantity cap, so B2B orders aren't blocked by a rule meant for retail shoppers.

Exploring

Minimum quantity rules

The inverse of a max-quantity rule — require a minimum order size on products where small orders don't cover fulfillment cost.

Exploring

Scheduled rules

Set a rule to activate and expire automatically — useful for time-boxed launches and short discount blackout windows.

Exploring

Rule analytics

"This rule triggered 45 times this month" — see which rules are actually protecting margin, and which ones to retire.

Exploring

Product-page notices

Show "max 2 per order" directly on the product page via a lightweight theme app extension, so customers see the limit before adding to cart.
