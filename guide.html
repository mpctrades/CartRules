Quick start

The fastest path from install to your first live rule.

  • Install CartRules from the Shopify App Store and open it from your admin's Apps menu.
  • Click New rule — the 3-step wizard opens (rule type → products → message).
  • Pick a rule type, choose the products it applies to, and write one line explaining it to customers.
  • Click Save & activate. It's enforced at checkout immediately — no theme edits, no code.

That's the whole loop. Sections 2–5 below walk through each part in detail with the exact screens you'll see.

02

The two rule types

CartRules version 1 has exactly two rule types. Knowing which one to reach for is most of the job.

A"No discount codes"

Excludes the products, collection, or tag you choose from every discount code at checkout. Use it for new releases, items already discounted, or licensed goods with fixed prices — anything that shouldn't stack a second markdown on top.

  • Blocks native Shopify discount codes on matching products
  • Shows your custom message instead of applying the code
  • Doesn't touch discounts on the rest of the cart
SUMMER15 does not apply to "Album Special Ed." — this item is excluded from promotions.

B"Max quantity per order"

Caps how many units of a product a customer can buy in one order. Use it for bulky or heavy items where a large quantity pushes shipping into a losing margin, or to protect a limited launch from being emptied by resellers.

  • Auto-adjusts the cart quantity down to the limit
  • Shows your message explaining why
  • Holds at cart, checkout, and after a quantity edit

One screen to see and control everything you've set up.

Rule · Applies to · Status

Every row shows the rule type, exactly which products/collection/tag it targets, and whether it's Active or Paused. Click a row to edit any of the 3 steps without recreating the rule.

  • Pausing stops enforcement immediately but keeps the rule's settings — reactivate any time.
  • Editing lets you change targeting or the message without losing rule history.

Three real setups, straight from the problems CartRules was built to solve. Copy the pattern for your own catalog.

07

Troubleshooting

Before contacting support, check these — they resolve the vast majority of "it's not working" reports.

The rule doesn't seem to trigger

Open the rule on the dashboard and confirm its status is Active, not Paused. A paused rule keeps its settings but enforces nothing.

A tag-based rule is missing some products

Double-check the exact tag spelling on the product (tags are case-sensitive) and that the product is published to the same sales channel where the order is placed.

The customer message isn't showing

An empty message field falls back to a default system message. Re-open the rule's step 3 and confirm your custom text was saved before activating.

A discount code still applies to an excluded product

Confirm the product is actually inside the rule's target (product, collection, or tag) and that the rule is Active. Codes added to the cart before the rule was activated may need to be re-applied.

I hit "3 active rules" on the Free plan

That's the Free plan limit. Pause a rule you're not using, or upgrade to Growth for unlimited rules — see plans below.

Plans & limits recap

Full pricing detail lives on the overview page — here's what actually changes as you upgrade.

  • Free — 3 active rules, both rule types, default messages, product/collection targeting.
  • Growth ($4.99/mo) — unlimited rules, custom messages in any language, tag targeting, email support.
  • Pro ($9.99/mo) — everything in Growth, plus product-page notices and priority support.
  • Billing lives under Plans & billing in the app sidebar — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time.
